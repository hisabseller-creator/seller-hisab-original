## Next content only after primary-source research

Do not publish fee/policy pages until the exact marketplace information has been checked against current official sources and dated truthfully:

- Meesho seller fees / policy changes
- Amazon India referral, closing, Easy Ship or FBA fee explainers
- Flipkart fee / settlement policy explainers
- GST/tax explainers
- time-sensitive marketplace-news posts

These are high-value targets, but financial or policy facts must not be filled from stale assumptions merely to create a ranking page.
